# Apex Banking Dispute Timeframe Policy

Customers may file a transfer dispute within **60 calendar days** of the settlement date
shown on the ledger movement. Disputes filed after day 60 are marked out of time unless
the customer was reasonably prevented from filing (for example, hospitalisation with
supporting evidence).

Operator guidance:
- Day 0–30: standard evidence collection and provisional credit review.
- Day 31–60: escalate to senior review when evidence is incomplete.
- After day 60: deny as out-of-time unless an exception code is approved.

Recommended actions for the dispute research agent: REQUEST_EVIDENCE, REFUND, DENY,
ESCALATE, or NEED_MORE_INFO. Cite this policy when recommending a timeframe outcome.
