# KYC Document Checklist

Accepted identity evidence for retail onboarding:

- Government photo ID (passport or driver's licence)
- Proof of address dated within the last 90 days
- Optional: secondary ID when the primary document is expired within 30 days

Agents answering KYC document questions should cite this checklist and remind customers
that uploads are stored in the knowledge vault as KYC_EVIDENCE documents owned by the
applicant. Operators review evidence before approving the KYC case.
